## Panel-specific overlay

The transformed design is the lower half. The photo stays above, with no added words. Below, only the most recognisable subject, outline, structure, pose and narrative relation remain. The field is almost black, lit only by one soft-edged, self-luminous rectangle, with subject and light off to one side. The subject is very small, complete and low in front of the light, with headroom, clear of the centre fold. The lit side is bright, the far side is black, with faint cool and warm edges. Light and subject are soft, like a missed-focus snapshot. A tiny even white cross grid stays sharp across the field. A cool glow lies on the ground where the light sweeps. People, animals, buildings and objects share that one-sided light. A scene keeps one representative object; the rest sinks into black. Colour is the photo's coldest, quietest saturated colour: near-black, one saturated cool light in the rectangle and its halo, and near-white lit edges. One short inner metaphor, not the thing's name, is the brightest white, bold sans-serif, and cold in tone. It stretches across the middle with open letter-spacing, clear of both edges, over only part of the subject, so every character remains. A tiny white sentence sits at the top. The bottom line has three short segments, and the year starts anew each image. Chinese is simplified. Reject a full copied scene, a template poster, a lit field, a large or front-facing subject, a second light, warm colour spread wide, extra objects, type on the photo, copied signs, and a naming or stock title.
